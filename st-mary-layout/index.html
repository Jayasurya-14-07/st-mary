About

Unit of Diocese of Jammu-Srinagar Education Society (DJSES)

St. Mary’s Convent School, Chhallan, Ramkot, is an English-medium, co-educational secondary school, affiliated to the Jammu and Kashmir Board of School Education (JKBOSE). The school was established in 2002 by the Catholic Education Society of the Jammu–Srinagar Diocese Correction: The school was established in 2002 by the Diocese of Jammu–Srinagar Education Society. Leadership Messages

Sr. Joy Maria DM

Principal

“Education is not merely the acquisition of knowledge; it is the formation of character, the awakening of potential, and the preparation for a meaningful life.”

It gives me immense joy to welcome you to the official website of St. Mary’s Convent School, Chhallan Ramkot.

Established in 2002, our school has grown with a clear vision of providing quality education rooted in values, discipline, compassion, and excellence. Over the years, St. Mary’s has become not merely a place of learning, but a nurturing home where young minds are guided to discover their talents, develop their confidence, and grow into responsible and caring individuals.

Utkarsh Singh

Head Boy (Student Council)

My journey at St. Mary’s Convent School began in Nursery, when I was a quiet and hesitant little child, taking my first steps into the world of learning. Little did I know that this school would become so much more than a place of education. It would become a second home—a place where I would discover my strengths, overcome my fears, build lasting friendships, and gradually find my confidence.

Over the years, I have grown alongside my school, both academically and personally. Every experience, whether a joyful achievement or a challenging moment, has taught me valuable lessons in patience, discipline, responsibility, and maturity. With the constant guidance of my teachers, I have learned to face difficulties with courage, understand different perspectives, and make thoughtful decisions rather than react impulsively.
