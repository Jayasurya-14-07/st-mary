Our Heritage & Journey

A Legacy of Quality Education & Values

Established in 1987, St. Mary’s Convent School, Chhallan, Ramkot stands as a beacon of wisdom and value-driven learning in the region of Jammu & Kashmir. Founded under the auspices of the Diocese of Jammu-Srinagar Education Society (DJSES), the school has dedicated nearly four decades to fostering academic brilliance and moral uprightness.

Our educational philosophy seamlessly merges modern digital pedagogies with timeless ethical principles, empowering students to grow into confident global citizens who shine with purpose — "Lighted for Life".
